# Measure the success condition and keep a margin

TASK.md states the task's success condition. It is the definition; your own margin against it is
something you can measure.

- **When success is detected.** The episode ends at the first moment the condition holds
  (`task_complete: true`). Recording what your perception said just before, and in failed attempts
  how far you were from the condition and in which direction, shows how close you run.
- **Aim for the middle, not the edge.** A result that only just meets the condition succeeds in one
  layout and fails in the next; aiming well inside regions and well past thresholds holds up better.
- **What is not required.** If TASK.md does not ask you to let go, move on, stay still or keep
  holding, time spent on it before success is detected is lost. If it does ask, doing it deliberately
  avoids surprises.
- **"Looks done" is not "is done".** A visual impression is not the condition; the measured quantity
  is (a height above the support, a position inside the target's extent, an angle).
- **Margins next to success counts** warn you of a shrinking margin before the count drops.
